Who We Are

A small studio with a big appetite for the unusual.

We started Pure Mix in 2017 with two screens, one client and a stubborn belief: that great design can be both daring and dependable. Today we're a team of ten designers, developers and storytellers making work we're proud to sign.

  • ✓Strategy-led — we solve problems before we pick colours.
  • ✓Full-service — brand, web, campaign and code in-house.
  • ✓Small by design — senior people on every project, no hand-offs.
